Amazing Cat Facts

😴

Sleep Champions

Cats spend 70% of their lives sleeping — that's about 13-16 hours every single day!

🏃

Lightning Fast

A house cat can reach speeds of up to 30 mph — faster than Usain Bolt's top speed!

👃

Super Sniffers

Cats have 200 million scent receptors in their nose, while humans only have about 5 million.

💤

Dream Weavers

Cats experience REM sleep just like humans — meaning they likely dream about chasing mice!

🎵

Healing Purrs

A cat's purr vibrates at 25-150 Hz — frequencies known to promote bone healing and reduce stress.
